## Project launch

OSS self-hosted Resend webmail over [poche](https://github.com/javimosch/poche). Go BFF + React CDN UI. MIT.

- **Multi-tenant mailboxes** — per-address auth, storage caps, aliases, forgot-password via Resend
- **SMTP receiver** — accept email directly for domains with MX pointing to your server
- **Storage usage bar** — live quota tracking in the sidebar
- **Retention & cleanup** — per-mailbox policy with starring to preserve messages
- **Reply support** — with proper Resend object/array email format handling
